# YARUN Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 2026-10-12
**Developer:** SRLabs
**Contact:** support@srlabs.kr

[한국어 보기](privacy-ko)

YARUN is a running app for Wear OS watches. This policy explains what data the app uses and how it is handled.

## Data the app uses

While you record a run, the app reads the following from your watch through Android Health Services:

- **Heart rate** (body sensor) – shown during the run, averaged in your run history, and used for the optional heart-rate vibration alert.
- **Location (GPS)** – used to measure distance and pace and to draw the route of your run.
- **Physical activity** (steps, cadence, movement) – used to measure distance, cadence, step count, and to pause automatically when you stop.
- **Barometric altitude**, when the watch has the sensor – used for the elevation chart.

The app also stores your settings (units, display mode, alert preferences).

## Where the data is kept

**All data stays on your watch.** Run history, samples, routes and settings are stored only in the app's private storage on the device. The app has no server, does not create an account, and does not send any data to the developer or to third parties. The app does not contain advertising or analytics SDKs.

Voice guidance uses the text-to-speech engine installed on your watch (for example Samsung or Google). Some engines may download voice data from their provider when you select a voice; that download is governed by the engine provider's policy, not by this app.

## Deleting data

- Delete a single run from **History** in the app.
- Delete everything by uninstalling the app, or by clearing the app's storage in the watch settings.
- Optionally, the **Keep history for** setting removes runs older than the chosen period automatically.

## Permissions

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| Physical activity | Required to record a run. |
| Location | Distance, pace and route map. Optional: without it, distance is estimated from steps and no route is drawn. |
| Body sensors / heart rate | Heart-rate display and alerts. Optional. |
| Notifications | The ongoing "recording" notification while a run is active. |

## Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal information.
